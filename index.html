Reports page: design iterations

Three design iterations of the Reports page. Open each one to compare, then choose the direction to take forward.

  • Iteration 1
    • Reports shown as a visual catalogue of covers
    • Open or download a report or its summary in one click
    • Simplest option with the least to learn
    • Best for occasional visitors who know what they want
  • Iteration 2
    • Everything in Iteration 1
    • Read a report alongside the catalogue without leaving the page
    • Adds "Ask MTM": ask questions about reports in plain language
    • Best for users who want quick answers without reading full reports
  • Iteration 3
    • See a report's key facts and summary before opening the full file
    • Save reports to a personal library and download them together
    • Includes "Ask MTM" for plain-language questions
    • Best for frequent users and researchers; more on screen at once

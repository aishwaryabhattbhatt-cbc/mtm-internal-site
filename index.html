Reports page: design iterations

Two directions for how people find, read and keep MTM reports. Open each one, try it as a user would, and tell us which to take forward.

  • Iteration 1

    Browse and ask. Reports are laid out as a catalogue of covers. Choose one to read it beside the catalogue without losing your place, or ask MTM a question and get an answer drawn from the reports. Familiar and quick for people who want to scan and dip in.

  • Iteration 2

    Research and collect. Reports are listed on one side with the key findings, summary and contents of the selected report on the other, so people can judge a report before opening it. Reports can be saved to a personal library and downloaded together. Built for people who use MTM research regularly.

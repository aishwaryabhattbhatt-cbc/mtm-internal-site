# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Internal MTM research and planning users.

## Product Purpose

Compare actual and forecast media-technology penetration across markets, with
options to export raw data or add it to YourData.

## Operating Context

Users select a product, market, and technologies, then compare year-by-year
actuals and forecasts. The supplied screenshots show a 2009–2031 timeline with
the forecast period beginning in 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The supplied interface shows a product selector, National/Anglophone/Francophone
market filters, technology filters, marker and value controls, legend placement,
raw-data export, and an Add to YourData action. For the local review variant,
chart values should be synthetic and clearly identified; technology-to-series
mapping is still open.

## Evidence on Hand

Two user-provided screenshots of the Forecasting Tool. No dataset or connected
data source was provided.

## Product Principles

- Keep market and technology selection central to forecast comparison.
- Make the transition between actuals and forecasts apparent.
- Identify synthetic prototype data so it cannot be mistaken for reporting data.
